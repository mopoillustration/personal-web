I’m so happy to have had the opportunity to be interviewed by VoyageATL and share a little more about my creative journey.

As a freelance illustrator, I find it rewarding to reflect on my experiences, including the challenges and discoveries along the way. I hope some of these reflections might offer encouragement or inspiration to other creatives at a similar stage.

This interview was also a lovely chance to share the stories behind my work. My illustrations often grow from personal experiences and small moments that mean something to me, and I’m grateful for the opportunity to put those feelings into words.
