**My series Self-Talk as Night Falls has been shortlisted for the World Illustration Awards 2026!** ✨

When I began this series, I was simply trying to give visual form to the quiet conversations I have with myself after dark. I’m so happy that these private, blue-toned moments have travelled this far.

I’d also like to share a little of the creative process behind the series here. Creating these pieces brought me a deep sense of stillness, and I truly enjoyed the entire process.

Thank you to the World Illustration Awards, the judges, the Association of Illustrators, and the Directory of Illustration for this recognition and support. 💙
