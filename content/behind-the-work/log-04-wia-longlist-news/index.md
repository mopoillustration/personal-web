I'm very excited to share that **my series Self-Talk as Night Falls has been longlisted for the World Illustration Awards!**

This series consists of five A4 pieces, created almost entirely with only three blue coloured pencils. It was inspired by my own experience of thinking, reflecting, and talking to myself when night falls.

Through two identical girls, quiet night scenes, and subtle surreal elements, I wanted to explore moments of inner dialogue, clarity, and calm.

Thank you so much to the World Illustration Awards, the AOI, the Directory of Illustration, and all the judges for recognising my work.

Thanks for reading and for your support.
Have a luminous day!
