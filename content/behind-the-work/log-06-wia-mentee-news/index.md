A new chapter begins. 

I'm delighted to be joining this year's AOl Mentorship Scheme, where I'll be mentored by the brilliant Jess Nash.

As someone building a career in editorial illustration and picture books, this opportunity means a great deal to me.

I'm incredibly thankful to the AOI for making this mentorship possible. Over the past few months, I've been quietly building my practice through self-initiated projects, mostly figuring things out on my own. Having the opportunity to learn from an experienced illustrator feels like a door opening to a whole new stage of the journey.

I'm excited to keep learning, asking questions, making new work, and sharing the journey along the way. :)
